1. Overview & Our Privacy Principles

Tracklet SaveBuddy ("we", "our", or "the app") is a modern, offline-first personal financial manager designed to help individuals track expenses, organize multiple ledgers ("Books"), manage categories, and monitor their personal investment portfolios.

We believe your personal financial data belongs solely to you. Our architecture is built upon four fundamental privacy guarantees:

  • Zero Advertisements & Trackers: The application contains no third-party ad networks, tracking SDKs, or data-collection brokers.
  • Offline-First Architecture: Your financial data is primarily stored on your physical device in an internal SQLite database sandbox.
  • Encrypted Remote Sync: Cloud synchronization communicates exclusively with our dedicated REST API over secure, encrypted HTTPS channels.
  • Full Data Autonomy: You retain complete rights to view, modify, and permanently purge your account and all associated transaction records at any time.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information directly necessary to provide core budgeting, portfolio tracking, and multi-device synchronization features:

A. Account Identification Data

  • Display Name: Provided during account registration to personalize your in-app profile.
  • Email Address: Used as your unique account username for authentication, password changes, and critical service notifications.
  • Password: Stored securely as a salted and hashed value on our server. Stored locally on your device within the encrypted Android Keystore for HTTP Basic Authentication.

B. Financial Records & Transactions

  • Ledger Books: Custom book titles, subtitles, and archive statuses created to organize different spending accounts (e.g., "Personal", "Work").
  • Expenses & Incomes: Transaction descriptions, monetary amounts, credit/debit indicators, transaction timestamps, assigned categories, and associated book identifiers.
  • Categories: Category names, custom icon identifiers, and color theme preferences chosen to classify your spending.
  • Investment Portfolio: Investment descriptions, recorded monetary amounts, and transaction dates.

C. Device & Local Preferences

  • Network Connectivity Status: Queried locally on your device via connectivity_plus solely to determine whether an active Wi-Fi or mobile network exists to execute two-way synchronization. Network status is never logged or transmitted.
  • Application Preferences: Locally stored user settings such as chosen display currency (e.g., USD, INR, EUR), theme mode (Light, Dark, System), and daily reminder notification preferences stored in local SharedPreferences.

Explicit Non-Collection: Tracklet SaveBuddy does NOT collect, store, or access: bank account numbers, credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, bank login credentials, government identification numbers (SSN/PAN/Aadhaar), biometric identifiers, physical GPS location data, or your personal contact address book.

3. How We Use and Store Your Information

All collected data is utilized exclusively for application functionality:

  • Local Database Storage: Your ledgers, expenses, and investment entries reside locally in a private SQLite database (expense_tracker.db) accessible only by the Tracklet SaveBuddy application process.
  • Hardware-Backed Credential Security: Account credentials and authentication tokens are stored in the Android Keystore using RSA OAEP and AES-GCM encryption provided by flutter_secure_storage. No plaintext passwords exist in unencrypted application preferences.
  • Cloud Synchronization: When connected to the internet, delta records are synchronized with our REST API server at https://132.226.191.34 to prevent data loss and ensure continuity across device reinstalls.
  • Transit Encryption: All client-server communication is strictly enforced over TLS 1.2/1.3 (HTTPS) with a trusted Let's Encrypt SSL certificate. Unencrypted cleartext HTTP traffic is blocked at the operating system level (android:usesCleartextTraffic="false").

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We maintain a strict policy against sharing or monetizing your information:

  • No Sale or Rental: We never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal or financial data to any third party, marketing network, or commercial broker.
  • No Third-Party SDK Integrations: Tracklet SaveBuddy bundles no third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics for Firebase, Mixpanel, or AppsFlyer) and no advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob).
  • Legal Requirements: We will only disclose account information if strictly required to comply with a valid legal obligation, court order, or enforceable governmental request under applicable law.

5. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion

Your financial records are retained on our servers only for as long as your registered account remains open and active.

A. In-App Instant Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all server-side records at any time directly within the application:

  1. Open Tracklet SaveBuddy on your device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings tab.
  3. Under the DATA & STORAGE section, select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete Permanently.

This action invokes our transactional deletion endpoint (DELETE /api/users/delete-account), which permanently deletes your user record and all associated expenses, investments, categories, and books in our cloud database, while simultaneously wiping your local SQLite database, Keystore credentials, and session state.

B. Web-Based Account Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your Android device, you can submit an external account deletion request through our public web portal at:

https://naman-punamiya.github.io/Tracklet-SaveBuddy/delete-account.html

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child without verified parental consent, we will take immediate steps to permanently delete that information.

7. Your Rights Under Data Protection Regulations

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play Data Protection Standards), you possess specific rights regarding your data:

  • Right of Access: You can inspect all books, expenses, categories, and investments directly within the app interface.
  • Right of Rectification: You can edit and update any transaction, book title, category, or profile name at any time.
  • Right of Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): You have the unconditional right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud records.
