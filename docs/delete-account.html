Account & Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, Tracklet SaveBuddy allows all users to request the permanent and irreversible deletion of their account and all associated personal and financial records at any time, including from outside the app.

Application: Tracklet SaveBuddy Package ID: com.namanpunamiya.expense_tracker Backend API: DELETE /api/users/delete-account

Permanent & Irreversible Action

Executing an account deletion permanently purges all your data from our database. This action cannot be reversed. Once completed, your transaction history, custom categories, investment portfolios, and financial ledgers cannot be restored.

What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, our systems execute an atomic, cascading purge of all records associated with your account identity:

Data Category Specific Records Included Action Taken
Account Identity Full Name, Registered Email, Hashed Passwords, Session Tokens Permanently Purged
Financial Ledgers All custom books, titles, subtitles, and archive markers Permanently Purged
Expenses & Incomes Descriptions, amounts, credit/debit flags, dates, tags Permanently Purged
Categories Custom category names, icons, and color assignments Permanently Purged
Investment Records Investment descriptions, purchase amounts, timestamps Permanently Purged
Local Cache & Storage SQLite tables, Android Keystore tokens, SharedPreferences Wiped Immediately (In-App)

Retention Policy: We do not retain "soft-deleted" records or inactive profiles. All database entries are permanently deleted via database cascade.

Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

If you currently have the Tracklet SaveBuddy application installed on your Android device, you can delete your account instantly without waiting for manual verification:

  1. Open the Tracklet SaveBuddy app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the DATA & STORAGE section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Review the prompt and tap Delete Permanently.

The app will authenticate with the server, execute DELETE /api/users/delete-account, wipe your local SQLite database, clear all encrypted Keystore tokens, and return you to the login screen with zero residual data.
