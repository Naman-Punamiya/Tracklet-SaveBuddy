Account & Data Deletion Request

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies on Account Deletion, users of Tracklet SaveBuddy may request the complete and permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal and financial data without needing to retain the app on their device.

⚠️ Warning: Permanent & Irreversible Action

Submitting this request will permanently erase your profile, all financial ledgers ("Books"), transactions, categories, investments, and synchronization records from our cloud database.

Option 1: In-App Immediate Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the app installed:

  1. Open Tracklet SaveBuddy on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to Account Management and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion. Your local SQLite storage and remote cloud data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: External Deletion Request by Email (No App Installation Required)

If you no longer have access to the app or have uninstalled it, you can request deletion of your Tracklet SaveBuddy account without installing the app by contacting NDevStudios (developer of Tracklet SaveBuddy) by email.

Support email (selectable/copyable):
ndevstudioshelp@gmail.com

Suggested email subject: Account Deletion Request - Tracklet SaveBuddy

If your device does not have a configured email client, you can copy the email address above and send your request from any email service.

How to request deletion:

  1. Send an email to ndevstudioshelp@gmail.com requesting deletion of your Tracklet SaveBuddy account.
  2. Ideally, send the request from the email address registered with your Tracklet SaveBuddy account so we can locate and verify the account.
  3. Support will review your request and verify account ownership before any deletion is processed.
  4. If you cannot access your registered email address, contact support and request an alternative ownership verification process.
  5. Once ownership and your intent to delete the account are confirmed, the account deletion is processed and you will receive confirmation through the support communication channel.

Important

Submitting an email request does not itself mean your account has already been deleted. Ownership verification is required before deletion. For your security, do not send passwords, Basic Authentication credentials, or other secrets by email.
