Account & Data Deletion Request

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies on Account Deletion, users of Tracklet SaveBuddy may request the complete and permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal and financial data without needing to retain the app on their device.

⚠️ Warning: Permanent & Irreversible Action

Submitting this request will permanently erase your profile, all financial ledgers ("Books"), transactions, categories, investments, and synchronization records from our cloud database.

Option 1: In-App Immediate Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the app installed:

  1. Open Tracklet SaveBuddy on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to Account Management and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion. Your local SQLite storage and remote cloud data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: External Web Deletion Request Form

If you no longer have access to the app or have uninstalled it, submit your deletion request below. We will verify your email and process permanent account deletion within 72 hours.
