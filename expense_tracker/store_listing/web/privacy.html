Google Play Compliance Disclosure

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Application: Tracklet SaveBuddy (Package: com.ndevstudios.trackletsavebuddy)

1. Introduction

Tracklet SaveBuddy ("we", "our", or "the app") provides offline-first personal financial ledger, budgeting, and investment tracking services. We are firmly committed to safeguarding your personal and financial information. This Privacy Policy details what data is collected, how it is handled, and your rights concerning your personal records.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your Name and Email Address to authenticate your identity across your devices.
  • Financial Information: We store the financial ledgers, expenses, incomes, categories, notes, transaction amounts, dates, and investment holdings you choose to record.
  • Device & Connectivity State: Network connectivity state is queried locally on device solely to queue and perform database synchronization.
  • No Sensitive Identifiers: We do NOT collect bank account numbers, debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, government ID numbers, or biometric information.

3. How We Use and Store Your Data

  • Offline Storage: Your entries are stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database.
  • Cloud Synchronization: When an internet connection is active, transaction entries are synchronized via HTTPS with our secure application servers to enable multi-device continuity.
  • No Advertising SDKs: Tracklet SaveBuddy contains NO third-party advertising SDKs, ad trackers, or affiliate marketing libraries. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties.

4. Data Security

All remote data in transit between the application and our servers is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Cleartext HTTP traffic is strictly prohibited on Android.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as your account remains active.

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated transaction records directly within the app by navigating to Settings > Account Management > Delete Account. This immediately wipes all local tables and issues a server deletion call.
  • External Deletion Request (Email): If you no longer have the app installed, you may request deletion of your Tracklet SaveBuddy account by contacting ndevstudioshelp@gmail.com using the instructions on the account deletion page: https://naman-punamiya.github.io/Tracklet-SaveBuddy/delete-account.html. The linked page explains the email request and ownership-verification process.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or compliance concerns regarding this policy, please reach out to our team at ndevstudioshelp@gmail.com.
